# Posbank — Training Brief

## Positioning (who they are)
Posbank is a South Korean POS hardware manufacturer (the brand of Posbank Co., Ltd.) specialising in fanless all-in-one POS terminals, self-service kiosks, self-checkout (SCO) systems, and kitchen display systems for retail and hospitality. Their flagship APEXA platform is an established industry best-seller, and the line is positioned as value-to-mid-tier: well-built, flexible hardware at strong channel pricing rather than a premium badge. Notably, Posbank is also an ODM that manufactures hardware for other POS brands (including Aures), which is why their build quality and reliability stand up against names that cost more. IO Resource carries the full Posbank range because it combines fanless reliability, a broad peripheral ecosystem (2nd displays, MSR, VFD, cash-drawer, fingerprint), consistent ex-stock availability through our warehouse, and channel pricing that lets us win mid-tier retail and hospitality deals on value. It is IOR's #2 brand overall (~20% of revenue) and the single largest brand in the departing rep's book (~35%) — a top priority to protect.

## Where it fits (verticals)
- **Hospitality (restaurants, QSR, cafés, bars, hotels):** Apexa counter terminals, Mint compact all-in-one, BigPos self-order kiosks, and KDS for the kitchen. Fanless = quiet and grease/dust-tolerant; IP54 front panel resists splashes.
- **Retail (convenience, specialty, franchise, grocery):** Apexa at the till, BigPos / Mazic for self-checkout and self-service, 2nd display for promos and loyalty.
- **Typical customer:** mid-tier multi-site retailers and hospitality operators, and the ISVs / POS software houses and resellers who deploy a software stack onto reliable, well-priced hardware. Posbank's positioning works best as a *platform* sold with a software partner, not as a bare hardware price fight.

## Sales talking points
1. **Fanless reliability.** No fan means no moving parts to fail, no dust/grease ingress, silent running and longer life — exactly what a 7-day hospitality or retail floor needs.
2. **One platform, many CPUs.** The same Apexa chassis scales from Intel N97 / Celeron J6412 for light retail up to Core i3 / i5 for software-heavy or busy sites — easy to right-size and easy to standardise an estate on.
3. **Built like the premium brands — because it builds them.** Posbank is an ODM that manufactures hardware for other POS brands (e.g. Aures), so customers get that build quality at better channel pricing.
4. **Complete ecosystem from one source.** Terminal + 2nd/customer display + MSR + VFD/pole display + cash-drawer + fingerprint + A8 printer — all Posbank, all from IOR ex-stock, one SKU set to support.
5. **Upgrade path & continuity.** Apexa Core / CX gives existing Apexa sites a drop-in modern refresh (newer Intel, 7H glass, FHD) without retraining or re-mounting — VESA-compatible throughout.

## Common objections + answers
- **"I've never heard of Posbank — is it a real brand?"** → Established Korean POS manufacturer; APEXA is a recognised industry best-seller and they ODM hardware for better-known brands. We hold stock and stand behind it.
- **"Fanless can't handle a busy kitchen / heavy software."** → Fanless is *more* reliable in hot, greasy, dusty environments, not less — no fan to clog. And you can spec Core i3/i5 versions when the software load is genuinely heavy; light retail runs happily on N97/J6412.
- **"It's cheaper than [Aures/NCR] — is the quality there?"** → Posbank actually manufactures product for premium brands, so the build is comparable; you're paying for the badge elsewhere, not better hardware.
- **"What about spares, peripherals and support?"** → Full peripheral range and accessories ship from IOR stock; tool-less maintenance, easy RAM/SSD swap, VESA-standard mounting, and CE/FCC certified.
- **"Will it run my POS software?"** → Yes — Windows 10 IoT / Windows 11 across the Apexa range (and Android or Windows on Mint), with the I/O ports POS apps expect (serial, powered USB, cash-drawer, MSR).

## Competitive / displacement angle
- **vs Aures:** Strongest angle of all — Posbank is the ODM that *builds* Aures hardware. Same manufacturing pedigree, typically better channel pricing, and IOR carries it ex-stock. When a customer is loyal to Aures, position Posbank as the same quality without the brand premium.
- **vs NCR:** NCR is the incumbent enterprise/tier-1 POS name with the price and lock-in to match. Posbank wins on **value, footprint and simplicity** for mid-tier retail/hospitality — a compact fanless all-in-one with a complete peripheral set at a fraction of NCR's cost and complexity, ideal for operators who don't need (or want to pay for) the NCR ecosystem.
- **General fanless POS pitch:** lead with reliability (no moving parts), total cost (lower hardware + fewer failures + ex-stock availability), and the single-vendor peripheral ecosystem. Sell the platform with a software partner rather than competing on bare hardware price.
